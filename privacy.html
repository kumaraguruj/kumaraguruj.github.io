Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers UnknownFacts Uploader, a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels @unknownfact-tamil and @unknownfacts-eng.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels and set their titles, descriptions and tags;
  • create and add to playlists on those channels;
  • read the channels' basic details and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained).

It does not access any other Google data, and it does not access anyone else's account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Analytics are used only to show the owner how the channels are doing and to plan future videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services, and use of YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on the owner's computer removes all stored tokens and reports.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through either YouTube channel above.
