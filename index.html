UnknownFacts Uploader

A private tool for the YouTube channels UnknownFacts Tamil (@unknownfact-tamil) and UnknownFacts English (@unknownfacts-eng).

UnknownFacts Uploader is a small personal program run by the owner of these two channels on their own computer. It does three things:

  • uploads the channel owner's own original science-story videos to their YouTube channels,
  • adds those videos to the channels' series playlists,
  • reads the channels' own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, subscribers) so the owner can see what works.

It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

Watch the stories on YouTube: @unknownfact-tamil · @unknownfacts-eng
